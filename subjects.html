Subject catalogue

24 areas to explore
01

Number

Decimals, fractions, place value, primes, factors, and order of operations.

 02

Algebra

Use symbols, expressions, formulae, and substitution to represent problems.

 03

Equations

Solve linear, simultaneous, quadratic, and graphical equations with confidence.

 04

Inequalities

Represent and solve inequalities using number lines, regions, and graphs.

 05

Ratio and Proportion

Compare quantities, scale relationships, and solve direct proportion problems.

 06

Percentages

Work with percentage change, reverse percentages, growth, and decay.

 07

Sequences

Identify patterns, find nth terms, and work with arithmetic and geometric rules.

 08

Graphs

Plot coordinates and interpret linear, quadratic, cubic, and reciprocal graphs.

 09

Coordinates

Use gradients, midpoints, distance, and equations of straight lines.

 10

Geometry

Understand angles, polygons, constructions, loci, and geometric reasoning.

 11

Transformations

Translate, reflect, rotate, and enlarge shapes on a coordinate grid.

 12

Pythagoras

Find missing sides in right-angled triangles and apply the theorem to problems.

 13

Trigonometry

Use sine, cosine, and tangent to solve triangle and angle questions.

 14

Circle Theorems

Explore angles in circles, tangents, chords, and intersecting lines.

 15

Vectors

Describe movement and prove geometric relationships using directed quantities.

 16

Area and Volume

Calculate measures for 2D and 3D shapes, including prisms and frustums.

 17

Measures

Convert units, estimate sensibly, and select appropriate levels of accuracy.

 18

Probability

Use sample spaces, tree diagrams, Venn diagrams, and conditional probability.

 19

Statistics

Compare data with averages, charts, scatter graphs, and measures of spread.

 20

Sampling

Understand populations, samples, bias, and how data can be collected fairly.

 21

Financial Maths

Apply maths to bills, wages, interest, exchange rates, profit, and loss.

 22

Indices and Surds

Simplify powers, standard form, roots, and exact values without a calculator.

 23

Quadratics

Factorise, complete the square, use the formula, and interpret parabolas.

 24

Proof

Build clear mathematical arguments with deduction, contradiction, and counterexamples.
